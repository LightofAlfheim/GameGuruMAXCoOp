HOW TO PLAY
===========

Double-click "Play". Then:

  Play           - play on your own.
  Join co-op     - play with a friend. Make sure Steam is open first.
                   You'll see a list of Steam friends who are hosting:
                   click the one you want to join.
  Host co-op     - you host. Friends click "Join co-op" and pick you from
                   their list (up to 3 friends can join).

When the game opens, start the same level as your friend.


Steam invites
-------------
Don't accept Steam invites for this game - they open a different game ("Spacewar").
Use "Join co-op" and pick your friend from the list instead.


If Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
--------------------------------------------
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". This happens because the game isn't from a big
publisher, not because something is wrong.


If it won't connect
-------------------
- "Your game files don't match the host's": you and the host have different versions,
  or game files were changed. Get the same copy of the game as everyone else.
- Check Steam is open and you're signed in, and that you and your friend are
  Steam friends.
- Check your friend clicked "Host co-op" and has started the level.
- Still stuck? Close the game and send your friend the file "coopnet-client.log"
  from this folder.
